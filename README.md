# >_GUN.exe

Turret control for EVE Frontier smart turrets: set who your turrets shoot (peace, war or hold fire, with ally and enemy
pilot and tribe lists) and apply it to every turret in one signature.

- **Hold** fires on no one. **Never** shoots you, your ally pilots or your tribe (unless a tribe mate is on the enemy list).
- **Peace** (default): other pilots only while they start an attack. **War**: every other pilot in range.
- Allied tribes are spared until they start an attack; listed enemies are always fired on.
- Contract: `0x406265ce7b62c5a7feda87a70b4cadbb5c65e89a916b2c209204f3ea8dcb0888` (module `turret`), testnet Stillness.
